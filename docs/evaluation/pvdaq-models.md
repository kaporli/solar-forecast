Candidates (fitted per fold on target days up to fold start minus two days, see `docs/evaluation-protocol.md`):

- `physics_calibrated`: the physics baseline times one least-squares factor on load factor.

## Limitations

- Validation spans 2024-11-13 to 2025-06-30: each season appears at most once; season-specific conclusions are not drawn.
- The ablation is not used for selection: removing lags or calendar inputs does not hurt here, but the selection is made among the three planned candidates only.
- Feature importance is not reported; the ablation shows what removing a group does here, not why.
- Historical scores are retrospective; operational claims need issuances recorded live (#6).
- One installation; these results do not validate the Belgian model or other sites.
