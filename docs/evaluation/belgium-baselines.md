## Limitations

- Validation covers target days 2024-11-13 to 2025-10-31, one year at most: each season slice comes from a single year.
- Physics uses assumed parameters (`config/evaluation.json`, physics), not fitted ones; calibration belongs to the learned-model comparison in #5.
- The fleet geometry and performance ratio are assumptions for the whole Belgian fleet; one point per province stands for each province.
- Elia forecasts are taken as archived in ods032 and ods209; whether they equal what Elia published at the time is unverified.
