## Limitations

- Validation covers target days 2024-11-13 to 2025-06-30, one year at most: each season slice comes from a single year.
- Physics uses assumed parameters (`config/evaluation.json`, physics), not fitted ones; calibration belongs to the learned-model comparison in #5.
- One installation: no result here says anything about other sites or about the Belgian aggregate.
- The test period is half a year (2025-07-01 to 2025-12-31); it will not cover a full season cycle.
